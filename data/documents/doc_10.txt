Source: https://docs.kernel.org/admin-guide/cgroup-v2.html

cgroup is a mechanism to organize processes hierarchically and distribute system resources along the hierarchy in a controlled and configurable manner. cgroup is largely composed of two parts: the core and controllers. cgroup core is primarily responsible for hierarchically organizing processes. A cgroup controller is usually responsible for distributing a specific type of system resource along the hierarchy, although there are utility controllers which serve purposes other than resource distribution. cgroups form a tree structure and every process in the system belongs to one and only one cgroup.
